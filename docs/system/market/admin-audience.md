# Admin app — audience

**Planning:** `docs/planning/admin/planning/app-prd.md`  
**Product:** Trip Admin Console (internal web)  
**Users:** Operations, Compliance, Finance, Ad Managers, Support, Super Admins

---

## Target user types

- **Compliance:** Document and profile approvals, flags, regulatory alignment  
- **Operations:** Live trips, driver online status, interventions  
- **Finance:** Wallets, payouts, reconciliations  
- **Ad Manager:** Taxi Assist Media campaigns and performance  
- **Support:** Tickets and rider/driver communication  
- **Super Admin:** Full access  

---

## Motivations

- **Speed** with accuracy — high verification throughput without mistakes  
- **Visibility** — queues, risk signals, audit trails  
- **Confidence** — actions are traceable (POPIA, disputes)  

---

## Pain points to respect in copy

- Queue overload — labels and filters must be **scannable**  
- Fear of wrong approval — confirmations and mandatory notes where PRD requires  
- Tool sprawl — prefer consistent terms with platform entities (rider, driver, vehicle, trip)  

---

## Emotional drivers

- Control and professionalism (not hype)  
- Calm precision under load  

---

## Expectations

- **Direct** UI copy — internal users tolerate density but not ambiguity  
- Errors: **what broke** + **what to do** (still no raw stack traces as the only message)  

---

## Language accessibility

- Operational English; acronym expansion on first use in docs or tooltips where helpful  
- Avoid patronizing tone — users are experts in their role  

---

## Voice note

APD-Market applies: **clarity and next action** still beat technical swagger. Prefer “Retry save” over “Persistence layer failure.”
