# Driver app — audience

**Planning:** `docs/planning/drivers/planning/app-prd.md` (driver mobile app)  
**App folder in repo:** `apps/driver_app`  
**Product:** Taxi Assist — driver mobile application  
**Market:** South Africa — Eastern Cape / Gauteng corridors initially (pilot)

---

## Target user types

- **Primary:** Registered driver with valid PDP, linked to an approved vehicle — wants maximum earning time with **clear compliance**.  
- **Secondary:** Private vehicle owner — registers vehicle and links drivers.  
- **Secondary:** Fleet / company owner — manages multiple vehicles and drivers.  
- **Note:** Back-office approval happens in admin — never imply instant approval in driver-facing copy unless product states it.

---

## Motivations

- **Earn** reliably from trips and visibility into fares  
- Stay **compliant** without surprise suspensions — renewal and expiry clarity  
- **Operate safely** with understandable trip states and rider interactions  

---

## Pain points to respect in copy

- Document rejection without clear **what to fix**  
- Ambiguous “online” vs “approved” states  
- Fatigue from long forms — progressive disclosure and encouragement  

---

## Emotional drivers

- Pride and professionalism (“You’re verified — go online when you’re ready”)  
- Reduced anxiety on expiry (“Renew before [date] to keep driving”)  
- Control over acceptance and earnings summaries  

---

## Expectations

- Honesty about **pilot corridors** and rollout  
- Clear separation: **your action** vs **waiting on review**  

---

## Language accessibility

- Plain English; explain SA compliance concepts **in rider/driver terms** (e.g. “licence and vehicle papers”) before acronyms  
- Short actionable lines on errors and document uploads
