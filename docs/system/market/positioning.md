# Platform positioning

**Aligned with:** `docs/planning/product-vision-and-concept.md`, `docs/planning/platform-architecture-and-ecosystem.md`

---

## What we are

**Taxi Assist / Trip** — a compliance-first, multi-sided ride-hailing platform for South Africa that connects riders with **verified** drivers, supports **wallet, card, and cash**, and powers **in-trip advertising** (rider-funded credits) with **internal operations** tooling for verification, safety, and campaigns.

---

## Audience-facing framing (outcomes)

| Audience | Emotional hook | Differentiation (user language) |
|----------|----------------|----------------------------------|
| **Riders** | Get where you’re going safely; pay your way; optional credits from ads during rides | Simple booking; flexible payment; transparency on driver and trip |
| **Drivers & owners** | Earn with a platform built around SA compliance | Clear steps to approval; documents and vehicles handled in one flow; earn from trips (and future media where applicable) |
| **Internal staff** | Keep the network safe and moving | One place to verify, monitor, and resolve — built for auditability and POPIA-aware handling |

Avoid promising specifics not in PRDs (corridors, fees, campaign rules). When in doubt, cite planning docs.

---

## Pilot reality

Rollout is **pilot-first** in defined corridors; messaging can mention **expanding service** without overclaiming national coverage unless product confirms.

---

## Words we lean on

- Verified, safe, simple, clear next step  
- Earn, book, go live, finish setup  
- Credits, wallet, trip — plain English  

## Words we avoid in rider/driver marketing

Internal jargon: RLS, NATIS as acronym-only without context, “workflow,” “module,” “execute.”

---

## Single line (brand test)

**We help people move and earn — with verification and payments that fit South Africa.**

Adjust per channel; keep claims aligned with `docs/planning/`.
