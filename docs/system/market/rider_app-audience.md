# Rider app — audience

**Planning:** `docs/planning/rider_app/planning/app-prd.md`  
**Product:** Taxi Assist — rider mobile app  
**Market:** South Africa (pilot corridors per platform docs)

---

## Target user types

- **Primary:** Registered rider — verified phone and email; books trips and manages profile, payments, and safety.  
- **Secondary:** Friends invited via referral — entering through promo or invite links.

---

## Motivations

- Reach destinations reliably with a **clear fare** and assigned driver  
- Pay with **cash, card, or wallet** without friction  
- Feel **safe** (driver visibility, emergency contacts, support)  
- Optionally **offset future trips** through in-trip media credits where rules allow  

---

## Pain points to respect in copy

- Uncertainty about arrival time, driver identity, or total cost  
- Payment confusion when wallet does not cover full fare  
- Interruptions during ads — copy should set expectations without sounding punitive  

---

## Emotional drivers

- Trust and control (“I know who’s picking me up and what I’ll pay”)  
- Relief after booking (“You’re booked — meet your driver”)  
- Fairness on ratings and tips  

---

## Expectations

- Registration asks for **personal details** — explain **why** in human terms (safety, verification), not legal boilerplate alone  
- Post-trip: mandatory rating — frame as quick feedback, not bureaucracy  

---

## Language accessibility

- **Plain English**; South African context (locations, payment habits)  
- Short sentences in onboarding and errors  
- Avoid technical terms for maps, payments, or ads
