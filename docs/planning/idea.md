Frontend:
The user(rider).
On the first slide the user will get an option to register or login in, they will login using a username and password they have previously created then if they want to register they will register using full first name and last name, current age, date of birth, ID number / passport number, sex,current residential address then select if its a stand alone or apartment, if its an apartment then they will have to fill in the the unit number and name of the complex. Then they will provide the current cell phone number which will be used as their username and also a one time pin will be sent to the number to verify it, providing a working email address that they use to activate their account from. Password for login in then password verification.

After logging into their account then the home page will appear where they can request trips from, this will also have a drop down menu of various tabs the rider has, such as the App wallet that will show the accumulated amount of money from watching ads. Taxi Assist Media, which will show them the total amount accumulated by watching adverts, number of adverts watched in total, amount available for using. My profile so they can change / add information and also use it to attach documents so they can get verified. Trips, this will show them their trips history this will give them access to contact the driver if they have forgotten something in the car. Payments, this will allow them to add multiple cards as a payment option. Emergency contact, they will add 5 relatives or friends that will be notified if the driver gets involved in an accident. invite friends, this will allow the rider to invite people using a promotion code, a link would be sent to them via sms or email. Support, this will give the user access to talk to our support team. Log out, this will give the rider the option to log out of their profile.

The rider will then be able to request a rider that will have a pick up location that will pickup the current riders location and also have the option to edit the pick up location, then the destination can be written in or choose location on the map. After the two locations have been inserted then it will show the estimated total amount of the trip. The rider can then confirm or decline the ride. If the client declines it will take them back to the home screen but if they confirm then they can select a payment method which will be app payment, card payment or cash payment, if they select app payment they need to select another option between cash or card payment option too so if the funds on the app payment are not enough to cover the amount then they will top it up using one of the methods. Then the app can allocate a driver to the rider.

Then it will show the drivers current location and estimated time it will take them to arrive, the drivers profile picture, full name and surname, car make and colour, and an option to contact the driver by calling or in app messaging. This will also have a part where the rider can update location if they want to change it at some point in the trip. Then when the driver arrives it will show the rider.
Once the driver starts the trip then the rider will get a pop up that suggests for them to watch adverts while on the trip in order to accumulate money for their next trip. Adverts will start playing then in order for the next advert to begin then they will need to rate the product being advertised out of 5 stars then leave a comment in order for the skip button to appear, this will also allow for the advert to be recorded as watched. If the rider decides to click on the advert while it's playing it should redirect the rider to the website of the product but will have to come back to the app and rate then comment on the advert in order to move to the next advert. If the client leaves the platform or the advertising screen or the trip is ended by the driver while an advert is still playing then that advert is not recorded as watched as it was not finished nor rated and commented on.

When the trip is ended by the driver then the client will see the total amount for the trip then

The client will then have to rate out of 5 stars and leave a comment on the trip experience. The tip option will appear if the ride was a card or app wallet payment. If using app payment then the tip cannot accede the maximum amount on the app payment wallet.

The last slide after the trip ends before the client can go back to the home screen would be the total amount accumulated by the rider while watching adverts that they can use on their next trip.

Then the home screen which will show the drivers earnings for the day and number of trips taken for the day, a tab that allows the driver to go online and offline as they please, a timer showing available hours to drive.
the driver can receive requests which will show them the pick up location, destination, estimated time to pickup, estimated time of the trip, estimated total of the trip, name of the client, verified or not verified and payment method.
